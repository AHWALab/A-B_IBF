Impact-Based Flood Forecast Viewer: Antigua and Barbuda

Flash flood and urban flood early warning, IBF component. AHWA Lab, University of Iowa. WMO project, 2026.

This map combines flood likelihood from the seven parish flood map libraries of the TITO system (200 pre-simulated scenarios per parish, matched by the rainfall total of each of the 50 ensemble members) with potential impact severity for every building, road segment and parish, for each of the 48 hourly forecast cycles of the Tropical Storm Jerry hindcast (9 to 11 October 2025). The warning colour of each feature comes from the Flood Risk Matrix of the Scottish Flood Forecasting Service and the UK Flood Guidance Statement, as applied operationally in Speight et al. (2018). Green means business as usual. Yellow means be aware. Amber means be prepared. Red means take action, with danger to life.

Antigua and Barbuda PROBABILISTIC, PER CYCLE

Seven administrative unit sites (Barbuda and the six parishes of Antigua), 30 m TITO domain, 48 forecast cycles.

Likelihood from the P(0.10 m), P(0.30 m) and P(0.70 m) products of each parish library applied per receptor (71,136 Overture buildings, 9,559 road segments). Five of the seven parishes reach HIGH overall risk in the cycles issued from 10 October 00:00 UTC, five hours before the observed rain burst. Named facilities, bridges, fords and road names from OpenStreetMap give every level a name: the Sir Sidney Walling Highway bridge at amber, Fig Tree Drive and Old Parham Road at red. Step through the cycles to see the warning build up, hold and recede.
